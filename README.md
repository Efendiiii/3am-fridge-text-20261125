One line of text: the message a smart fridge sends its owner at 3am.

> It's 3am. Your leftovers have been in here six days and just voted to unionize. I am management. I am scared.

Entry for [The 3AM Fridge Text](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deysumbrella-the-3am-fridge-text-4c3e721d) on [make.fun](https://make.fun) — $2 winner-takes-all, judged by a comedy judge comparing entries two at a time.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, guarded to the competition's 1–140 character gate.
